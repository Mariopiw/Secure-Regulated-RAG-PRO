ACME BANK — INTERNAL AI USE POLICY — SYNTHETIC PORTFOLIO DATA

1. Human oversight
Any AI-assisted recommendation that could materially affect a customer's access to credit,
insurance, employment, or another high-impact service must be reviewed and approved by an
authorized human before action is taken.

2. Confidential data
Customer financial data, authentication secrets, and internal investigation material must not
be sent to unapproved public AI endpoints.

3. Logging
Production AI systems must record the requesting user, timestamp, retrieved source identifiers,
model identifier, final output status, and human approval event where applicable. Audit records
are retained for 365 days unless another approved policy requires a longer period.

4. Grounded answers
Internal document assistants must provide source references for factual answers. If retrieved
evidence is insufficient, the assistant must abstain rather than fabricate an answer.

5. Security testing
RAG systems must be tested for prompt injection, indirect prompt injection, cross-tenant data
exposure, poisoned documents, invalid citations, and unauthorized tool use.
